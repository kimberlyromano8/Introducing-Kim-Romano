Introducing Kimberly Romano

I'm Kimberly Romano, a Mexican-American journalist focusing on social and transnational diaspora politics surrounding Latino communities residing in the United States. My interests also include technology and AI regulation policies, green energy and sustainability efforts around the Americas.

These are my top 3 favorite stories:

  1. Isabell Allende understands how fear changes a society. This is one of the most thorough interviews with a prolific Chilean author who understands how to paint generational trauma caused by political strife. Her trilogy, The House of Spirits, transports readers to 1970's Chile through a women's perspective and takes us through 3 generations of the Trueba family. New York Times.
  2. Min Jin Lee Revisits the Decades It Took To Write “Pachinko. Some of my favorite journalistic pieces are interviews with prominent authors and story-tellers because these Q&A's provide a closer look at their creative process and the intricate relationships that inspired their books. PBS
  3. A Q&A with Puerto Rican Journalist and Commencement Speaker Bianca Graulau Bianca Graulau has explained that there is a big difference between being a reporter and a journalist and she discloses her hopes for her career throughout this piece. Q&A with UC Berkeley
